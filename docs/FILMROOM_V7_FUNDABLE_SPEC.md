## 1. Executive Summary

Film Room is a football competition platform built for fantasy leagues, creator communities, and high-engagement fan groups.

The core idea remains:

**turn weekly football talk into a structured game with standings, rivalry, and repeat behavior.**

V7 keeps the strongest V6 decisions:

- the group is the unit of retention
- the commissioner or creator host is the unit of activation
- League Pass is the first clean monetization unit
- creator leagues are a core growth wedge
- content trust is the central product risk

But V7 addresses the remaining weak spots:

1. content authority is now explicit and real, not aspirational
2. the competitive landscape is addressed directly
3. the creator GTM is more concrete
4. the offseason strategy is more fully designed
5. the scale economics go beyond League Pass

V7 focuses on credibility.

## 3. Core Thesis

Film Room is not:

- a trivia app
- a fantasy management app
- a broad fan education tool
- a generic sports companion

Film Room is:

**the competitive football layer for existing fan groups.**

Its job is to give leagues and communities a recurring way to settle who actually knows ball.

The company wins if:

- hosts start leagues
- members care about the outcomes
- scenarios feel legitimate
- the group comes back weekly

Everything else is secondary.

## 4. Primary Customer And User

### Primary customer

The organizer:

- fantasy commissioner
- creator host
- community admin

This is the user who:

- invites the group
- creates recurring energy
- is most likely to pay
- takes social ownership of the experience

### Primary end user

League members and audience members ages 21-40 who:

- watch NFL weekly
- actively participate in football chat
- care about status
- enjoy structured competition
- are willing to spend 5-10 minutes on a weekly side game

### Why this target is right

The product does not need all fans.

It needs dense football groups with an organizer and strong existing identity.

That is a better wedge than broad football fandom.

## 6. Product Definition

### One-sentence definition

Film Room is a weekly football competition platform where leagues and communities play curated football scenarios, challenge rivals, and compete for standing across the NFL week.

### Core product loop

1. host creates a Film Room league
2. members join via invite or creator/community entry point
3. the league receives a curated weekly challenge
4. members compete for league standing and head-to-head wins
5. Sunday Live gives members a timed chance to gain ground
6. weekly results lock, callouts are generated, and next week resets

### Core surfaces

- League Hub
- Weekly Challenge
- Head-to-Head
- Sunday Live
- Profile

### Explicitly cut from launch focus

- Daily Challenge
- Practice Mode
- broad public social feed
- generic educational framing

## 7. Why This Wins Against Existing Alternatives

This needed a direct answer.

Film Room is entering a crowded behavioral environment.

Users already have:

- Sleeper
- ESPN
- Yahoo
- group chats
- creator content
- casual pick'em games

So why does Film Room exist?

### 1. It is not fantasy utility

Sleeper and Yahoo help users manage rosters and league logistics.

Film Room is not trying to replace lineup management.

It adds a different layer:

- football reasoning competition
- standings based on football judgment
- rivalry outside fantasy results

That can coexist with fantasy products because it serves a different emotional job.

### 2. It is not passive content

Creator videos and football analysis are consumed, not played.

Film Room turns football analysis into structured participation.

### 3. It is not just chat

Group chat is free and sticky, but it is unstructured.

Film Room formalizes the competition:

- shared challenge object
- scoring
- standings
- rivalries
- weekly winners

The group chat does not disappear.

Film Room gives the chat something to orbit around.

### 4. It is not generic prediction

Pick'em products are mostly about outcomes.

Film Room is about football judgment:

- what do you see
- what should happen
- what is the right read

That is a different identity category than betting or pick'em.

### Bottom line

Film Room wins only if users feel:

**"This is the legitimate way our group competes on football intelligence."**

If it feels like a weaker add-on to products they already use, it loses.

## 8. The Product Architecture

## 8.1 League Hub

The League Hub is the center of the product.

It includes:

- standings
- pending challenges
- rivalry board
- recent results
- host recap tools
- invite flow
- week status

### Launch league types

- Private Fantasy League
- Creator League

These are the two best wedges and should receive the best product experience.

## 8.2 Weekly Challenge

The primary shared game object.

### Format

- 5-7 scenarios
- 5-8 minutes
- consistent weekly rhythm
- one clear scoring lock

### Purpose

- creates the shared ritual
- anchors standings
- gives legitimacy to the league

## 8.3 Head-to-Head

The strongest emotional and viral mechanic.

### Purpose

- creates obligation
- drives rematches
- gives users a direct reason to re-open

### Launch functionality

- send challenge
- accept challenge
- compare results
- view rivalry record

## 8.4 Sunday Live

The retention accelerant.

### Launch format

Two reliable windows:

- early Sunday slate
- primetime window

### Goal

Test whether Sunday creates:

- higher reactivation
- more challenge behavior
- stronger league energy

### Product rule

Reliability matters more than cleverness.

If Sunday Live is unstable, it harms trust.

## 8.5 Profile

Profile should remain lean.

It includes:

- current standing
- season record
- rivalry record
- notable wins

## 9. Scenario System And Quality Bar

### Core content principle

The scenario should test football reasoning, not memory.

### What a scenario should do

- create tension
- present a defensible question
- allow fair scoring
- produce a satisfying explanation

### What it should not do

- reward users for remembering a famous play
- depend on obscure football jargon
- feel like AI-generated nonsense
- feel like arbitrary coach-room minutiae

### Content model

#### Editorial core

Hand-authored, coach-reviewed base scenarios.

#### Structured variants

Bounded scenario variations generated from approved frameworks.

#### Set packaging

League, creator, rivalry, Sunday, and offseason packs assembled from the approved library.

### Launch content requirements

- 100+ approved scenarios
- review pass by content lead
- validation input from ex-Ravens coach
- at least one creator-customizable pack format
- full 10-week content schedule before launch cohort begins

This is still a heavy requirement, but it is a real one.

## 10. Creator GTM Program

V7 makes the creator wedge more concrete.

### Creator target profile

The best early creators are not celebrity pundits.

They are:

- football creators with 25K-250K engaged followers
- strong Discord, Substack, podcast, or group-chat behavior
- audiences that enjoy debate and participation
- creators willing to run a recurring challenge

### Why this segment

- reachable
- less expensive
- more collaborative
- stronger audience density
- better early iteration partners

### Launch creator program

Goal:

- recruit 5 creator partners for the first season cohort

Offer:

- white-glove league setup
- co-branded competition page
- custom weekly creator pack
- recap assets for social and Discord
- direct feedback channel with founding team

### Creator outreach motion

1. identify 25-40 target creators
2. manually rank by engagement density and audience fit
3. offer founder-led onboarding
4. run small pilot with top 5
5. measure week-over-week participation and audience pull

### Success criteria

- creator launches league
- audience joins
- creator repeats weekly
- creator sees Film Room as additive to their community identity

This is a real GTM motion, not just a channel idea.

## 12. League Churn And Recovery

This remains a critical section.

### Why leagues die

- users fall behind
- no one pushes the group
- late joiners feel irrelevant
- standings become static

### Recovery systems

#### Weekly wins

Season loss does not mean current irrelevance.

#### Rivalry wins

Users can still beat someone directly.

#### Underdog mechanics

Bottom-ranked users can earn meaningful upside for showing up.

#### Host rescue tools

Hosts can nudge, summarize, and reignite the group quickly.

#### League health monitoring

Internal classification:

- healthy
- slipping
- at risk
- dormant

This should drive intervention logic.

## 13. Offseason Design

The offseason can no longer be treated as a vague placeholder.

### Offseason objective

Retain the highest-intent leagues and creator communities between NFL seasons.

### Offseason competition modes

#### 1. Draft Prep Season

- rookie evaluation prompts
- roster fit scenarios
- offseason team-building debates

#### 2. Historical Film Room Seasons

- curated all-time or recent-season competition packs
- rivalry seasons
- playoff replays framed as reasoning contests

#### 3. College Football Bridge

- selective college football competition packs
- especially useful for creator communities and year-round football fans

#### 4. Event-driven competitions

- draft week specials
- schedule release packs
- training camp packs

### Offseason principle

Do not aim to retain the entire user base.

Aim to keep:

- creators warm
- commissioners engaged
- top leagues active

That is enough to reduce September restart risk.

## 14. Monetization And Scale Economics

This needed to be more honest in V6.

League Pass is the right launch monetization model.

It is not enough on its own to create a venture-scale business.

### Launch monetization

#### League Pass

- $30-50 per league per season

Best for:

- fantasy leagues
- private groups
- creator beta leagues

### Additional revenue layers required for scale

#### 1. Creator monetization

- paid creator competitions
- premium audience tournaments
- creator revenue share

This can create higher ARPU than ordinary private leagues.

#### 2. Branded competitions

- sponsored weekly challenges
- season-long brand-backed competitions
- football-media partnerships

#### 3. Premium event formats

- playoff tournaments
- draft season competitions
- invitational leagues

#### 4. Enterprise or media partnerships

Possible later path:

- creator network partnerships
- football media properties
- communities and publishers

### Honest revenue framing

League Pass proves willingness to pay.

Creator and branded layers are what create the chance at real scale.

That should be said clearly in the fundraising story.

## 15. Competitive Landscape

This section is intentionally direct.

### Direct competition

- Sleeper social and fantasy ecosystem
- ESPN prediction and streak products
- Yahoo fantasy and pick'em layers
- creator communities running competitions informally

### Indirect competition

- group chats
- Discord debates
- football podcasts and breakdown shows
- betting-adjacent prediction products

### Why incumbents do not automatically win

Large incumbents are strong at:

- distribution
- utility
- scale

They are weaker at:

- focused community identity
- creator-led custom competition
- football-intelligence-specific gameplay
- fast iteration in niche interaction design

### Why Film Room can still fail

If the product is merely:

- another pick'em layer
- another prediction product
- another content app

then incumbents will crush it.

The company only works if it becomes:

**the legitimate competition layer for groups that want to prove football intelligence, not just game outcomes.**

That is the only defendable position.

## 16. Metrics And Proof Bar

### Critical activation metrics

- % of hosts who create league and send invites
- invite acceptance rate
- Week 1 challenge completion rate

### Critical retention metrics

- Week 3 league survival
- Week 5 league survival
- average active users per live league
- Sunday Live participation rate
- head-to-head rematch rate

### Critical trust metrics

- scenario legitimacy score
- % of users calling the experience fair
- % of users describing product as competition, not trivia

### Critical GTM metrics

- creator partner activation rate
- creator audience join rate
- repeat creator participation

### Critical monetization metrics

- League Pass conversion
- revenue per active league
- creator monetization pilot performance

### Practical proof bar for funding

Film Room becomes much more credible if it can show:

- 10+ non-fake leagues alive by Week 5
- 50%+ strongest-cohort week-over-week league retention
- one creator-led league with repeat weekly participation
- strong scenario trust from active users
- evidence that hosts will pay

This remains the real test.
