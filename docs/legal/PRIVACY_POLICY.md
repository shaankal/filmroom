# Film Room Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

Film Room is a football competition app for private leagues, creator communities, and fan groups. This policy explains what information Film Room collects, how it is used, and the choices users have.

Contact: support@filmroom.app

## Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate the app:

- Account information: email address, username, authentication identifiers, and optional profile details.
- League information: league names, invite codes, memberships, commissioner status, standings, rivalry records, and league settings.
- Gameplay information: scenario responses, response times, scores, results, H2H challenges, Sunday Live results, and profile statistics.
- Purchase information: League Pass status, purchase platform, transaction identifiers, and entitlement state.
- Notification information: push tokens or notification delivery records when notifications are enabled.
- Technical information: device, app, log, diagnostic, and crash information if enabled through platform or service-provider tooling.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create and authenticate accounts;
- create, join, administer, and score leagues;
- calculate standings, H2H records, rivalry history, and profiles;
- deliver notifications and commissioner nudges;
- process and verify purchases and entitlements;
- debug, secure, and improve Film Room;
- respond to support requests;
- comply with law and enforce our terms.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to operate Film Room, including hosting, database, authentication, purchases, notifications, app distribution, analytics, and support. These may include Supabase, RevenueCat, Expo, OneSignal, Apple, Google, and deployment providers.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or Film Room, or in connection with a business transfer.

## Payments

Mobile purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. Film Room does not store full payment card numbers.

## Children

Film Room is not directed to children under 13. Users under 13 may not create accounts or use the service. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact support@filmroom.app.

## Account Deletion

Users can request deletion in the app from Profile > Delete account. Deletion removes login access, leaves active leagues, deletes notification records, and anonymizes profile details while retaining league integrity records such as scores and standings.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, when appropriate, providing in-app or email notice.
